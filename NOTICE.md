## Why this exists

Elementary-school teachers routinely spend their own money on classroom supplies.
This template was created to give a teacher a simple, free, professional-looking
way to share a supply wish list as Amazon affiliate links. When the site owner is
an approved Amazon Associate, qualifying purchases earn a small commission at no
extra cost to the buyer. The intent is that those commissions go into a
**classroom fund** — covering hosting costs, supplies that weren't donated, and
small extras like an end-of-year celebration.

## Who it's for

- **Primarily:** elementary-school teachers and the people helping them (a
  tech-savvy parent, a friend, a school volunteer). Teachers deserve all the help
  they can get, and this is one small piece of it.
- **Also:** anyone who wants a clean two-page affiliate-link site as a starting
  point. The template is deliberately a good base for a **first-time affiliate**.
