Affiliate Disclosure

A short note about the affiliate links you'll find here.

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

What this means

Some links on this site point to products on Amazon. When a link is an affiliate link, clicking it and making a purchase may earn us a small commission, at no additional cost to you. The price you pay does not change.

Affiliate revenue goes directly toward classroom supplies and the small costs of running this site.

Where affiliate links appear

Affiliate links appear on the donations page, where each classroom-supply item is paired with a link to the matching product on Amazon.

How product details are shown

Where available, product images and prices are retrieved automatically from the Amazon Product Advertising API. Prices shown on Amazon at the time of purchase always apply.
